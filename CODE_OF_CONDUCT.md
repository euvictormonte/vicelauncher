# Code of Conduct

Neves Launcher is a community project. We want people to be able to ask, build, disagree and help without fear of abuse.

## Expected

- Be respectful and specific. Criticise code and decisions, not people.
- Assume good faith, and say so when you are unsure.
- Credit the work you build on, including the upstream Mandyhub and VIctory projects.
- Keep discussion about the project. Do not post private information about anyone, including their name, address, account or files.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, threats or discrimination, in issues, pull requests, chat or anywhere else around the project.
- Posting someone else's private data, or game files or copyrighted material that is not yours to share.
- Asking for, or sharing, ways to break someone else's systems or accounts.
- Presenting a fork or a copy as the official Neves Launcher, or as affiliated with VIPortLive, Mandyhub, Rockstar Games or Take-Two.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues or pull requests, or block accounts that break these rules. For a conduct concern, contact the maintainers through a private channel shown on the repository's profile, not in a public issue.
